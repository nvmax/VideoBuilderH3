H3 Studio 0.1.13 lets you choose what to import into the overlay library, shows an agent's edits to the timeline at once, and adds four blend modes and the fonts installed on your computer.

- **Choose what to import.** Overlays > Import folder first reads the folder and opens a screen of tiles, four across, with each pack's picture and its name. Point at a tile and a short preview plays: the pack's clip, joined with its matte when it has one, over a backdrop with the blend the import would use. Tick the ones you want (or Choose all shown), search by name, and press Import chosen. Only those are copied. Earlier versions copied every video in the folder.
- **Folders of effect packs.** For a folder of ready-made effect packs (a `filter.xml` in each pack), the screen lists the packs marked Overlay that the app can use. A clip with a matte becomes one transparent clip. A clip that only needs a blend (screen, overlay or multiply) is copied as it is, with that blend suggested, and you can change it in the clip's settings. Packs in a format the app cannot use yet (key color clips, flv, an unknown blend) are counted and left out, so nothing is copied that would not work. What you imported is remembered and is not listed again while its clip is in the library. Any other folder lists its videos the same way.
- **Your fonts.** Text clips and lyric captions can use any font installed on the computer. Overlays > Font for text and captions lists the app's own fonts, the font files in your overlay library and the installed fonts, and the choice applies to new text and to Add lyric captions. Font collections (`.ttc`) and symbol fonts are not listed.
- **Four more blend modes.** Darken, hard light, color dodge and color burn join the overlay blend modes (11 in all), in the player and in the export.
- **Agent edits show at once.** Transitions, effects and filters that an agent adds, changes or removes through the API or MCP now appear on the timeline within a moment, even while a scene renders. Before, the window waited for the render to end and gave up after about ten minutes, so the blocks were in the export but not on screen. Blocks you changed and have not saved are kept, and the window tells you when an agent changed the same list. The agent activity list says which lanes changed.
- **Empty lanes are hidden.** The Transitions, Effects and Filters lanes are drawn only while they hold a block, and go when you delete the last one.

Installed 0.1.12 offers this update itself: Menu, Check for Updates. Nothing installs without your click, or while a job is running. New installs: download `H3-Studio_0.1.13_x64-setup.exe` for Windows, or `H3-Studio_0.1.13_amd64.AppImage` or `H3-Studio_0.1.13_amd64.deb` for Linux below.
