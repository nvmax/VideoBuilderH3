H3 Studio 0.1.29 makes exports with effects and filters faster and cleaner, finds ComfyUI's output folder when you start ComfyUI after the app, stops edits from being lost when a render finishes at the same time, and fixes two things in the node check and the agent tools.

- **Effects and filters keep the video.** A stitch with effects or filters used to decode and encode every frame of the song again, at a lower quality than the scenes, even for a one second effect. Stretches without an effect are now copied as they are, from one keyframe to the next, and only the frames next to an effect are encoded again. What is encoded uses the same quality and preset as the scene clips. A copied piece is checked, and encoded instead if it does not come out exactly right. Scenes carry one keyframe at their start, so the copy runs scene by scene: the part of a scene around an effect is still encoded again.
- **ComfyUI after the app.** If ComfyUI was not running when the app started, its output folder stayed unknown until you restarted the app: projects in that folder were not listed and the Builder kept its profiles and defaults in the project folder. The app now asks again in the background and picks the folder up as soon as ComfyUI answers. The app also waits at most 1 second for ComfyUI when it starts, instead of 4.
- **Edits are not lost when a render ends.** A render saving its clip into the project, a picture job saving its image and a settings change are now each one step that no other edit can land in the middle of. Before, an edit made in the same moment could be overwritten, or the render's save refused after its clip was already written. Scene renumbering and restoring a scene video wait for other edits too.
- **Cancel and restart.** Video and picture jobs now remember the ComfyUI prompt they started. If the app is stopped while a job runs, the next start cancels that prompt in ComfyUI when ComfyUI is still running it, so it does not keep using the GPU for a result nobody will collect. Prompts that ComfyUI does not list are left alone.
- **Time left in jobs.** `GET /jobs` and the job events now give `eta_seconds` for renders and picture jobs. It was always empty before, with the time only in the message text.
- **Faster ComfyUI calls.** The app reuses one connection setup for its calls to ComfyUI, including uploads and the pass-through the Builder uses.
- **Node check.** The startup check now lists `MiniMaxH3SigmaShift`, the ComfyUI node that sigma shift needs, so an older ComfyUI is told to update. It is optional: the app still reports ready without it.
- **Agent tool.** `reference_image_generate` was listed for agents but always answered "Tool not found". It now generates the picture of a character or location card.

No pack update is needed. Installed 0.1.28 offers this update itself: Menu, Check for Updates. Nothing installs without your click, or while a job is running. New installs: download `H3-Studio_0.1.29_x64-setup.exe` for Windows, or `H3-Studio_0.1.29_amd64.AppImage` or `H3-Studio_0.1.29_amd64.deb` for Linux below.
