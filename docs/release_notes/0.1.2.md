Makes it much clearer what an AI agent is doing in your project, and fixes selecting part of a stem.

- **Agent activity.** The panel says when an agent connects. Click any row to see the API calls it made, what it was told and answered, and which scenes and fields it changed. Calls that only look at the project are dimmed, and repeats are merged into one row.
- **Real render progress.** A render, a picture or the lyric timing now shows ComfyUI's own step and the time left ("Pass 1 sampler step 12 of 30, about 2:10 left") instead of a percentage that stayed at 30%. A batch or a full video build moves through all its scenes. Work that has no way to say how far it is shows a clock. The percentage is an estimate.
- **A scene an agent renders looks like one you render.** It glows in the timeline, its card in the scene list shows the progress, and Follow selects it.
- **Stem selection.** Dragging over a stem now shows the green selection from the first click and follows the pointer until you let go.

Installed 0.1.1 offers this update itself: Menu, Check for Updates. Nothing installs without your click, or while a job is running. New installs: download `H3-Studio_0.1.2_x64-setup.exe` below.
