H3 Studio 0.1.26 adds your own thumbnail and intro video, the API image engine and Browser AI (Flow, GPT Image, Meta AI) to the Thumbnail dialog, and brings in the latest Video Builder pack changes.

- **Your own thumbnail and intro video.** The Thumbnail dialog takes any picture (png, jpg or webp) and any intro video you already have. The final video then starts with the thumbnail as its first frame, then your intro video with its own sound, then the video. An intro video of a different size or frame rate is fitted with black bars, never cropped.
- **Browser AI thumbnails: Flow, GPT Image and Meta AI.** Each is its own entry in the engine list. The app drives your own Chrome itself, with your own signed-in accounts: it uploads the frame you chose, types the prompt and brings the picture back. Nothing else needs installing (no Node.js, no Playwright). Click **Open Chrome and sign in** once for each site you use and leave that window open. Each site has its own window and sign-in, and if one fails the others are not tried. Flow was run end to end on the real site, and GPT Image and Meta AI were run from the Thumbnail dialog.
- **API image engine.** The Thumbnail dialog can also draw with the image API of the LLM Runner's provider (Gemini, Grok or OpenAI) with that provider's key.
- **Image panel Browser AI.** The Image panel's Browser AI routes are passed on to ComfyUI, where the pack's nodes run them. This needs the pack installed in the ComfyUI the app uses, on the same computer.
- **Builder front end.** The Video Builder window is updated from the pack: Auto Build scene presets with Medium shots as the default, and multi-shot Whisper timing before the LLM prompts.
- **Still not in the app.** The Overlay thumbnail engine.
- **Needs the matching pack update** for Browser AI in the Image panel and for the thumbnail choices that go through ComfyUI. Update the pack and restart ComfyUI.

Installed 0.1.25 offers this update itself: Menu, Check for Updates. Nothing installs without your click, or while a job is running. New installs: download `H3-Studio_0.1.26_x64-setup.exe` for Windows, or `H3-Studio_0.1.26_amd64.AppImage` or `H3-Studio_0.1.26_amd64.deb` for Linux below.
