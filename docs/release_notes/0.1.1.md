Fixes the Story Builder's locations, following the pack's latest Beta2.0 changes.

- Scenes tab: a scene's Spot is now a list of your location cards. Picking one maps the scene to that card. A planned spot that has no card yet stays as written until you pick one.
- Planning scenes maps them to any existing location card, including cards the Reference Builder edited (those could lose their story link, which left scenes without a location). A card keeps the story link it has.
- Auto Map follows the spot planned for each scene, and leaving the Reference Builder updates the plan's spots to match the mapping.

Installed 0.1.0 offers this update itself: Menu, Check for Updates. Nothing installs without your click, or while a job is running. New installs: download `H3-Studio_0.1.1_x64-setup.exe` below.
