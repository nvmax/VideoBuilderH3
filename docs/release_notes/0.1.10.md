H3 Studio 0.1.10 lets agents see a RefMod card as a RefMod.

- **RefMod cards in the scene cards agents read.** The scene cards that the agent API and the MCP tools return, and the story summary that the arc and brief steps read, used to show a RefMod subject or location as a plain picture card. They now carry the card's RefMod (`source` and `refmod`, with the saved RefMod's name, type, kind and strength), the way the Storyboard screen does.

Installed 0.1.9 offers this update itself: Menu, Check for Updates. Nothing installs without your click, or while a job is running. New installs: download `H3-Studio_0.1.10_x64-setup.exe` for Windows, or `H3-Studio_0.1.10_amd64.AppImage` or `H3-Studio_0.1.10_amd64.deb` for Linux below.
