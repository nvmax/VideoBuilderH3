H3 Studio 0.1.23 can refine pass 2 of a long 2 Pass scene in overlapping windows instead of one pass over the whole clip, and the scene render result now says whether it did.

- **Pass 2 in windows.** Two-Pass Settings has a new Long Scenes section with "Chunk pass 2" (on by default) and "Longer than (seconds)" (default 8, from 1 to 15 in steps of 0.5). A scene whose rendered clip is longer than that limit refines pass 2 in overlapping 3 s windows, so pass 2 no longer has to hold every frame of the scene at once. A shorter clip renders in one piece, as before. Set the limit to 3 or 5 to window shorter scenes. The length compared is the rendered clip, which includes the warm-up of a continued scene and H3's frame padding, so a 7.6 s scene can count as 8.4 s.
- **Where it applies.** Reference to Video and RefMod scenes in 2 Pass, with or without DMAD, sigma shift and masked continuation (the first part of a continued scene stays locked to the previous scene). Image to Video, single pass and 2 Pass Advanced render as before.
- **Needs the matching pack update.** The windows run in a new ComfyUI node, `VRGDG H3 Chunked Pass 2`, from the Video Builder pack, so update the pack and restart ComfyUI. Without that node the app renders pass 2 in one piece and the render result says to update the pack. The required-nodes check lists the node under "Pass 2 in windows for long scenes".
- **For agents.** The scene render result has `chunked_pass2` (`enabled`, `windows`, `rendered_seconds`, `reason`), so an agent can confirm what pass 2 did. `h3-cli render` prints the reason after a render. The two settings are in the settings schema, and the music video playbook says what to try when a scene runs out of memory in pass 2: lower "Longer than" to 5 or 3 before changing the pass 2 size.

Installed 0.1.22 offers this update itself: Menu, Check for Updates. Nothing installs without your click, or while a job is running. New installs: download `H3-Studio_0.1.23_x64-setup.exe` for Windows, or `H3-Studio_0.1.23_amd64.AppImage` or `H3-Studio_0.1.23_amd64.deb` for Linux below.
