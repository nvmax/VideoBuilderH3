H3 Studio 0.1.12 fixes two bugs found by oneevil, in pull requests #5 and #6.

- **Story Arc and lyric tags with a colon.** A section tag such as `[Verse 1: Male Rap]`, `[Chorus: Female Vocals]` or `[Outro: Male Voice]` made Story Arc fail with "could not preserve the lyric-section structure", even after the automatic format retry. The reply's heading was cut at the first colon, so `Verse 1: Male Rap` was read as `Verse 1`. A heading with its own colon is now read whole, in both the `Label:` line and the `[Label]: text` forms. Lyrics without a colon in their tags behave as before.
- **RefMod cards and the token total.** A Reference Builder card copied its RefMod's kind, tokens and frames when you picked the RefMod and never read them again. A RefMod saved again under the same name (rebuilt with fewer frames, another quality) left the card with the old numbers, so the scene status showed a wrong total and a false "Over 8,192" warning. The cards now take the current numbers from the saved RefMods when the scene status is worked out and when a card's RefMod list loads. Strength and the other card settings are kept. A card whose RefMod is no longer on disk keeps what it has and still says it was not found. The project is saved when a card changed.
