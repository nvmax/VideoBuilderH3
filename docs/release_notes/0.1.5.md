H3 Studio now runs on Linux, and the timeline has an Export button that saves the finished video where you choose.

- **Linux.** An AppImage and a `.deb` for 64-bit Linux, signed and updated like the Windows installer. They need `ffmpeg` (the `.deb` installs it) and `zenity` or `kdialog` for the Browse buttons. `bash build_installer.sh` builds them from source. The first Linux release: please tell us what does not work on your system.
- **Export.** The new button next to Scenes Locked turns on when every scene has its final video, and says what is missing while it is off. It opens a Save dialog, stitches all scenes with the project's audio exactly as Render All does, and saves the video there. Closing the dialog stitches nothing.
- **An open Builder follows agent renders.** A scene an agent renders now appears in the open window, with its video, history and glow, with no reload. Your own unsaved edits in the window are kept.
- **Theatrical cue sentences.** Timed vocal cues keep the acting prose of the sentence they are in, the Theatrical presets ask for a distinct physical and facial acting beat in every shot, and mouth-only directions are removed without losing who acts next. These come from the pack's latest Beta2.0 and apply to the Builder and to agent prompt jobs.
- **RefMod previews in the Storyboard.** A RefMod card shows its preview as the thumbnail in the scene chips and the subject picker (from oneevil).
- **Safer folder checks.** Every "inside this folder" check now resolves `..` the same way on every system.

Installed 0.1.4 offers this update itself: Menu, Check for Updates. Nothing installs without your click, or while a job is running. New installs: download `H3-Studio_0.1.5_x64-setup.exe` for Windows, or `H3-Studio_0.1.5_amd64.AppImage` or `H3-Studio_0.1.5_amd64.deb` for Linux below.
