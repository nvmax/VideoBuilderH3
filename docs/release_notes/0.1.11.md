H3 Studio 0.1.11 adds transitions, effects and filters to the timeline.

- **Transitions.** 143 transitions, as blocks on a lane under the overlay lane: 18 of the app's own (zoom pulls, whip pans, spin blur, glitch, VHS, light leak and more) and the 125 of the open gl-transitions collection. Open Post Process > Transitions and add one at the playhead. A block on a cut blends one scene into the next, a block inside a scene is an effect on its footage. Drag a block to move it, drag its edges to stretch it, double-click it to change it. It snaps to scene edges and beats.
- **Effects.** 78 effects on a lane of their own (glitch, camera shake, zoom pulse, blur, distort, glow, flash, CRT, VHS, halftone, edge detection and more), in Post Process > Effects. Each block has a strength and the effect's own settings.
- **Filters.** 126 filters on a lane of their own (cinematic, film, black and white, warm, cool, moody, vivid, pastel, portrait, food, landscape, music video), in Post Process > Filters, and `Adjust` for a grade of your own. Whole video... puts a filter over the whole timeline.
- **Picker and player.** The picker shows every look drawn on a real frame of your scene, with search and categories. The player draws a block live while the playhead is inside it. Delete removes the selected block and undo covers every change.
- **Same frames, same song.** A block never changes the number of frames, so the video keeps the timeline's length and the song stays in step. On a frame, filters are applied first, then effects, after the transitions and before the overlays. Render All, Export and the stitch draw them on the GPU (DirectX 12 or Vulkan, the software adapter when there is none).
- **For agents.** `GET /transitions`, `/effects` and `/filters`, `GET/PUT /projects/{pid}/transitions`, `/effects` and `/filters`, `POST .../set`, and `transitions`, `effects` and `filters` in the stitch request. They are saved in the project as `scene_transitions`, `video_effects` and `video_filters`.
- **Text to video 2 Pass, and built-in audio in every mode.** MiniMax text to video now has Single and 2 Pass, as in the pack. Built-in audio works in every mode and pass, Image to Video 2 Pass and 2 Pass Advanced included: pass 1 generates the sound and the later passes keep it. A pass chosen through the settings API now counts as a deliberate choice, so an old text to video save still opens on Single but your edit sticks.

The effects and filters are the app's own work. The transitions use the gl-transitions collection (MIT license, in the app's licenses).
