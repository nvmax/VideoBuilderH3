H3 Studio 0.1.7 lifts the limit on subjects and caps each scene at 8 references.

- **No limit on subjects.** A project can hold as many characters, props and places as it needs. The old limit of 12 dropped the 13th one every time the project was saved.
- **At most 8 references per scene.** A scene sends at most 8 Reference Builder images, or 8 RefMods in the RefMod pipeline (it was 24). The scene start frame and the continuity frame come on top of the 8, up to the 9 images H3 takes. A scene that maps more shows **TOO MANY** on its References button, and creating its prompt says how many to unmap. Agents get the first 8, and `over_limit_by` in `GET /projects/{pid}/scenes/{sid}/minimax-references` says how many were left out.
- **RefMod token warning from 8,192.** It was 6,000. Above it, the scene's status line says the scene may not render or may crash. Nothing is blocked.

Installed 0.1.6 offers this update itself: Menu, Check for Updates. Nothing installs without your click, or while a job is running. New installs: download `H3-Studio_0.1.7_x64-setup.exe` for Windows, or `H3-Studio_0.1.7_amd64.AppImage` or `H3-Studio_0.1.7_amd64.deb` for Linux below.
