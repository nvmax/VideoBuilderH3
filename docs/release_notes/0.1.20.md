H3 Studio 0.1.20 brings back drag and drop in the app and makes the Reference Builder fill its box.

- **Drag and drop works in the app.** Dropping image files onto the subject and location slots in the Scene Reference Builder and onto the sections in RefMod Studio did nothing, and neither did dragging a card to reorder it. On Windows the app's window was taking over drag and drop before the Builder could see it. The window now leaves it to the Builder, in the main window and in the pop-out windows.
- **The Reference Builder fills its box.** The Subjects list stopped at a fixed height and the Mapping list at 560 pixels, so they scrolled inside themselves while the box still had free space below. The Subjects and Mapping lists now take the height that is left, and the Extra Subjects and Locations panels reach the bottom too. A window too short for that scrolls the tab as before. This applies to the Scene Reference Builder window and to the Story Builder's Cast and Places tabs.

Installed 0.1.19 offers this update itself: Menu, Check for Updates. Nothing installs without your click, or while a job is running. New installs: download `H3-Studio_0.1.20_x64-setup.exe` for Windows, or `H3-Studio_0.1.20_amd64.AppImage` or `H3-Studio_0.1.20_amd64.deb` for Linux below.
