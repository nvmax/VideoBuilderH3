H3 Studio 0.1.9 makes the Storyboard show the current Reference Builder card of a scene.

- **A RefMod location or subject shows as a RefMod in the Storyboard.** A scene's location thumbnail could show an old picture and no RefMod preview or badge while the Reference Builder already had a RefMod on that card. The Video Builder now sends the RefMod with the card.
- **A changed card is not replaced by its saved copy.** When a Storyboard reopens, the saved copy of a scene's subject or location used to win over the Reference Builder's card whenever the id matched, so a picture changed after the first save, or a card turned into a RefMod, kept its old look. The Reference Builder's card now wins, and the saved copy only fills in what the card does not have.

Thanks to @oneevil for the fix.
